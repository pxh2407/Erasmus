WP2 — Closer to the European Union

Educational workshops for senior citizens · 4 modules, one per country

Work Package 2

WP2 consists of educational workshops on EU topics designed for senior citizens. Each of the four partner countries develops three workshops covering different aspects of the European Union.

Three workshops and related exercises, simulator and photo gallery are already available.
