WP3 — Voices from the Past, Visions for the Future

Panel discussions and seniors' testimonies · 4 countries

Work Package 3

WP3 is dedicated to the collection of senior citizens' memories and testimonies about their countries' journey towards and within the European Union. Each partner country organises a panel discussion, bringing together senior citizens to share their personal experiences of European integration. Reports will be published here as the activities take place.
