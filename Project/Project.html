Project Goals

General Objectives

  • Strengthen a sense of European identity among senior citizens and other participants.
  • Encourage seniors' social activation and participation in public life.
  • Develop innovative educational materials about the European Union.
  • Collect and publish seniors' memories of their countries' entry into the EU.
  • Promote dialogue between generations through public debates and workshops.

Specific Objectives

  • Develop 12 workshops (3 per country) on European topics for senior citizens.
  • Produce a methodological guide for workshop facilitators.
  • Organise panel discussions to collect seniors' testimonies about European integration.
  • Create and publish "Our European Journey: Voices from the Past. Visions for the Future".
  • Arrange 4 study visits to EU institutions in Brussels and Strasbourg.
