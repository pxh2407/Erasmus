2. Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you open a page, GitHub automatically records your IP address for security purposes and to keep the service running. We do not have access to this data. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies and browser memory

The website uses no cookies and no statistics or advertising tools. It only stores two preferences in your browser, so you do not have to choose them again: the language and the text size. They never leave your device and you can delete them by clearing your browser data.

4. Links to other websites

The website contains links to Facebook, to the partners’ websites and to Google Translate. These websites open only if you click on them and have their own privacy policies.

5. Photos and testimonies

Photos of the workshops and the testimonies of the participants are published only with the consent of the people concerned. If you appear in a photo or a text and want it removed, write to lutemilazzo@gmail.com: we will remove it as soon as possible.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data and object to its use, by writing to lutemilazzo@gmail.com.

You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (in Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
